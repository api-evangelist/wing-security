Artificial intelligence is no longer limited to chatbots and productivity assistants. Across organizations, employees are building, deploying, and sharing AI agents that can access business data, make decisions, trigger workflows, and act on behalf of users. What started as experimentation has quickly evolved into a new operational layer inside the enterprise.
